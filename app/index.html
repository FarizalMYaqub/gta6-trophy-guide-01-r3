    Roadmap to Platinum

    Five stages, in the order that wastes the least time. The story comes first because it unlocks the map, the activities and the robbery tools. The long grinds come last, when you can do them in free roam without story pressure.

    1. Stage complete

      Stage 1: Play the story, watch the one missable

      Play Chapters 1 to 4 and the final mission at your own pace. Before Chapter 1 ends, meditate at Lucia's balcony spot. It counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and the spot is gone after the chapter. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Do not use cheats: they disable trophies.

      Covers: all chapter trophies, the final mission gold, and the missable meditation spot.

    2. Stage complete

      Stage 2: Side characters and strangers

      Finish the mission strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, then take on three more stranger strands to reach 8 for Strangers and Freaks. Hunt the unique animal for Bret while you are out in the wild.

      Covers: the five side character finals, Strangers and Freaks, Fair Game.

    3. Stage complete

      Stage 3: Activities grind

      Work through the races, the drone races, the shooting range golds and the fishing challenge with Cal. Stack stunts toward 250 and clear challenges toward 50 as you go. Try one of each activity type for Master of None and finish an arcade game.

      Covers: Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash Not Kudos, Baited My Tackle, Stick Fiddler, One Last Thing, Gunslinger.

    4. Stage complete

      Stage 4: Robberies and money

      Rob the jewelry store undetected, five bank vaults, an armored truck and one illegal operation per gang. Launder every Marked Bill through your contacts toward $250,000, spend $100,000 in shops, buy all the garages, and register 10 vehicles to the Scooter Bros app.

      Covers: all robbery trophies, Dime on the Dollar, Treat Yo Self, Little Boxes, On the Spot.

    5. Stage complete

      Stage 5: Collectibles, secrets and 100 percent

      Sweep the map for the card sets, the 100 WAiNK It spots, the 100 vehicles and every region of Leonida. Chase the cryptic hidden trophies: the tank, the showroom smash, the bowling alley, the cocktail on Ocean Beach and the story riddles. Then open the in-game 100 percent checklist and clear whatever it still shows. The platinum pops when the last item does.

      Covers: A Long Road, the collectibles, the cryptic hidden trophies, and the platinum itself.

    Missable Warning

    Only one trophy is confirmed missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free roam.

    Positive Mental Attitude: meditate at every spot across Leonida

    There are 18 meditation spots. One of them is Lucia's balcony spot in Chapter 1, and it disappears when the chapter ends. There is no mission replay in GTA 6, so missing it locks the trophy for that playthrough.

    • Meditate at the balcony spot as soon as you can in Chapter 1.
    • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, and one before the final mission.
    • Do the other 17 spots at any time. They stay available after the story.

    Tips Before You Start

    No online trophies. All 55 unlock in single player. No PS Plus, no co-op partner and no online mode are needed.
    Cheats disable trophies. Turning on a cheat switches trophies off, and you cannot save while a cheat is active. Finish the platinum hunt first, then play with cheats.
    Expect a long hunt. The story alone runs about 60 hours, and the full list takes roughly 200 hours. It is long, not hard: there are no difficulty trophies and no mission medals.
    Save by hand. Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter and one before the final mission. There is no mission replay, so a save is your only way back.
    Some guides are still being confirmed. A few hidden trophies have joke descriptions and no published method yet. This guide says so plainly where that is the case, and cites the sources it used, instead of inventing steps.
